Privacy policy

Your name is public. Everything else isn’t.

Effective October 26, 2026

Who we are

Musk2028 is an independent grassroots movement. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Elon Musk, the America Party, America PAC, Tesla, SpaceX, X Corp., xAI, or any campaign. We do not collect money of any kind.

What we collect when you sign

  • Full name (required).
  • ZIP code and state (optional).
  • Email address (optional).
  • Your choices: whether you opted in to updates and that you agreed to the petition statement.
  • X account (only if you choose “Verify with X”): your X user ID and public username. We request read-only scopes, use them once to identify your account, and then revoke the access token. We never post on your behalf and never see your password or messages.
  • A one-way hash of your IP address, used only to limit abuse (rate limiting). We do not store the raw IP address.
  • Timestamp of your signature.

We don’t use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or third-party pixels. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

What is shown publicly

Only your first name, last initial, state (if given), and an X-verified or email-verified badge. Your email, ZIP code, full last name and X username are never shown publicly. Totals on the site are the real count from our database.

How we use it

  • To count and display support for the petition.
  • To confirm your signature by email, if you gave an email and email confirmation is enabled.
  • To send occasional movement updates, only if you opted in. Every update will include a way to unsubscribe.
  • To present aggregate counts, and if the petition is delivered, a list of signer names and states, to members of Congress, state legislators, or Elon Musk. We will not deliver email addresses.

We never sell or rent your information.

How to remove your signature

  • Right after signing, use “Remove my signature” on the petition (the option stays available in that browser).
  • If you gave an email and received a confirmation email, use the removal link in it.
  • Or contact the organizers and we will delete your record.

Removal permanently deletes your row from our database.

Retention and security

We keep signatures for as long as the petition campaign is active and delete them when it ends. Data is stored in a database on our server; access is limited to the organizers. Exports require an admin key.

Children

The petition is intended for people 13 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from younger children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above. We won’t use information you already gave us in a materially different way without asking.
